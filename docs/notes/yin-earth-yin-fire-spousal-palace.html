Frequently Asked Questions

Why do Ji Earth and Ding Fire Day Masters disproportionately experience caretaker burnout?

Porous garden soil (Ji Earth) absorbs surrounding emotional anxiety while focused starlight (Ding Fire) radiates finite warmth outward; without Metal boundaries or Wood/Fire replenishment, Output-heavy Day Pillars sustain chronic over-giving.

Which Ji Earth and Ding Fire Day Pillars receive nourishment at home rather than giving it all away?

Ji Si and Ding Mao sit directly atop Resource branches that feed the Day Master, whereas Ji You, Ji Hai, Ding Chou, and Ding Wei sit atop Output or Wealth terrain that defaults to outward caretaking.

Are Ji Earth and Ding Fire compatible with each other in BaZi synastry?

Yes, when seasonal temperature is balanced. Ding Fire gently warms cool Ji Earth soil, though dry summer pairings require Metal and Water outlets so emotional closeness stays breathable.
